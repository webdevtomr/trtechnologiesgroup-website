01 / The context

Domain knowledge, made useful.

RM Safe brings thirty years of workplace safety knowledge to a connected website and software product. The project spans the public-facing presence and the tools behind it.

That combination matters: the website introduces the offering, while the product gives the work a practical interface.

02 / The experience

Show the system behind the service.

The homepage places the software beside the proposition. Its contractor-management preview shows a searchable register, summary counts and compliance statuses, giving visitors a concrete view of the product.

The navigation separates information about the business from pricing, enquiries and sign-in. Red accents connect the website’s calls to action with the product interface.

01 / In the detail

A product in plain sight

The opening pairs the safety and HR proposition with the contractor-management interface rather than an abstract technology illustration.

02 / In the detail

Information with hierarchy

Summary counts, search and a structured register organise the contractor preview. Status labels distinguish compliant, expiring and expired records.

03 / In the detail

A connected offering

The website introduces document resources and software tools, including incident, employee and contractor management.
