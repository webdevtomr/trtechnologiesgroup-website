Hello Today

A daily check-in app that makes it easier for people living alone and their families to stay connected.

Project focus
Product design & mobile app development
Sector
Family & wellbeing
In the world
Explore Hello Today ↗ (opens in a new tab)

01 / The context

A small action that matters.

Hello Today gives people living alone a simple way to let family know they are okay. A daily check-in helps the people who care about them know when to get in touch.

02 / The experience

Simple enough to become a habit.

The app centres the everyday task: a clear check-in, large actions and a family view. Warm orange and a calm background make the experience feel approachable, with the important next step easy to find.

One clear check-in

A prominent “I’m okay today” action keeps the daily interaction straightforward.

A connected family view

Family members can see the check-in and know when to follow up.

Designed for everyday use

Readable text, generous controls and simple navigation keep the app focused on the people using it.
