01 / The context

Care, with a human touch.

Kith Grove Healthcare is a paediatric practice with a human approach to care. Its website introduces the practice, its character and the information families need to find.

The project is a welcoming digital home for a business that meets children where they are.

02 / The experience

A gentler first impression.

A handwritten identity and leafy graphic sit within a restrained green palette. The opening introduces the practice through community, care and growth.

Alongside that welcome, the navigation gives practical information a clear place: fees, frequently asked questions, contact details and a prominent referral route.

01 / In the detail

A human identity

The handwritten wordmark and organic leaf detail establish a different tone from a conventional clinical website.

02 / In the detail

Practical information

Fees, FAQs and contact information are visible in the main navigation, alongside the practice’s story.

03 / In the detail

An obvious referral route

Make a Referral is visually distinct in the navigation, helping the next action stand apart from general information.
