Nana Bread

A dedicated storefront.
A distinct personality.

Sector
Food & retail
Work
Website & ecommerce

The context.

Nana Bread is a bake-to-order bakery in Perth. With orders outgrowing direct messages, the project brings the business into a dedicated storefront.

The work.

The website gives the bakery’s personality room to show up while making it clear how to explore the range and start an order. Products, wholesale and catering, ingredients and the bakery’s story each have a place. The ordering route stays prominent throughout.
