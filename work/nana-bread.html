Nana Bread

A bake-to-order bakery that had outgrown orders by direct message. A digital storefront with as much personality as the product.

Project focus
Web design, ecommerce & development
Sector
Food & ecommerce
In the world
Visit live website ↗ (opens in a new tab)

01 / The context

A little slice of happy.

Nana Bread is a bake-to-order bakery in Perth. With orders having outgrown direct messages, the project brings the business into a dedicated storefront.

The website gives its personality room to show up, alongside a clear way to explore the range and start an order.

02 / The experience

Personality, with a place to go.

The storefront pairs warm yellow and brown with a playful wordmark. Product, wholesale and catering navigation sit alongside the bakery’s story, while the Order Now action remains prominent.

The result is a website that presents the bakery as a business and keeps the product’s character in view. Design and ecommerce are part of the same experience.

01 / In the detail

A recognisable welcome

A large wordmark, smiling face and warm colour palette introduce the bakery before the visitor reaches the product range.

02 / In the detail

A clear next step

The Order Now action appears in the navigation and the opening section, making the ordering route easy to locate.

03 / In the detail

Room for the business

Products, wholesale and catering, ingredients and the bakery’s story each have a place in the navigation.
