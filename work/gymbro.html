GymBro

A connected coaching platform for the people writing the programs and the people putting in the work.

Project focus
Product design, app & platform development
Sector
Fitness & coaching
In the world
Explore GymBro ↗ (opens in a new tab)

01 / The context

More than a workout log.

GymBro connects coaching tools with the training experience. Coaches can manage clients, build programs and review progress, while athletes have their training and check-ins close at hand.

02 / The experience

One connected experience.

The platform brings programming, messages, check-ins and progress into the same workflow. A restrained charcoal and lime identity gives the interface a clear visual language across the coaching dashboard and mobile app.

Built around the coach

Client management, programming and follow-ups give coaches a place to organise the work between sessions.

Made for the session

Training plans and workout logging bring the program into the athlete’s daily routine.

Progress in context

Check-ins and training history keep the conversation connected to what the client is doing.
