03 — Process

How a project actually runs.

Two people, one project at a time, no handovers

  1. 01

    Conversation

    One call

    What the business is, who the customer is and what is not working. No brief template, no discovery deck. By the end of it you know whether we are the right studio and we know whether the project is real.

  2. 02

    Scope and a number

    Within a week

    We write down exactly what is being built, what is deliberately not, and what it costs. Fixed scope, fixed price. If the scope changes later you find out what that costs before we build it.

  3. 03

    Structure and words

    Before any design

    Positioning, page structure and the actual copy come first, because a layout designed around placeholder text is a layout designed around nothing. You see and approve the argument before you see a picture of it.

  4. 04

    Design and build

    The bulk of it

    Designed and engineered in the same room by the two people you have been talking to. You see it working on a real URL as it goes, not as a flat picture at the end.

  5. 05

    Launch and after

    Ongoing, if you want it

    We put it live, hand over something you can edit, and stay reachable. Software projects continue in stages from here rather than stopping at a launch date.

Larger software builds run the same way but in stages: scope a stage, build it, put it in front of real users, then decide on the next one. You never commit to the whole thing before you have seen part of it working.

05 — Money

How we price, before you ask

So you can decide whether the hour is worth spending

01

Fixed scope, fixed price

We scope the work properly and quote a number for it. You are not billed by the hour and you are not surprised at the end. If the scope changes, you find out what that costs before we build it.

02

One conversation gets you a number

A call and a look at what you already have is usually enough for us to tell you the range, what we would do first, and what can sensibly wait until later.

03

We will tell you when not to spend it

If the honest answer is that you need a smaller version, or a different kind of help, or nothing at all yet, that is what you will be told. It costs us a project and saves you a year.

04

Websites and software are priced differently

A marketing website is a defined piece of work with a defined end. Operational software and SaaS platforms are scoped in stages, so you can see something working before committing to the whole build.
