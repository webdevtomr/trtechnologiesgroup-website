GROVE HEALTHCARE

We're two Perth speech pathologists who kept asking the same question: why does healthcare so often feel transactional? Kith Grove is our answer, built on connection, compassion and collaboration.

Kith is an old word for the people you know and trust. That's what we're building: a practice where you get straight answers to the questions keeping you up at night, and where the people caring for your family actually know them, not just their file.

with kith, with care
We are committed

Our mission

A grove is a stand of trees that grow stronger together, sheltering each other while they do it. We're building exactly that: a place where families get answers, kids find their voices, and the clinicians doing the work feel backed rather than burnt out.

Speech pathology and playgroups today. Workshops, mentoring and a family-clinician community platform as we grow.

Meet the team

The people behind the grove.

Sallina founded Kith Grove, and Daphne is the other half of it. Two Perth speech pathologists who wanted care to feel personal rather than processed.

Sallina

Founder • Speech Pathologist • CPSP

Sallina is a speech pathologist with over 13 years' experience, specialising in paediatric feeding, oral-motor development and early communication. She works with babies and young children, providing evidence-based, family-centred care across hospital and private practice settings.

Sallina is the founder of Kith Grove and is passionate about supporting families through the perinatal and early childhood period. She works closely with parents and perinatal health professionals to spot concerns early and give clear, practical strategies that fit into everyday routines. Her areas of interest are infant and toddler feeding, early oral-motor development, starting solids, and early communication.

Outside of work you'll usually find her with her multilingual baby, or running a Korean café with her partner.

Speaks English and Vietnamese.
Qualifications
  • Master of Speech Pathology, Curtin University, 2013
  • Certified Practising Speech Pathologist (CPSP), Speech Pathology Australia

Daphne

Speech Pathologist • CPSP

Daphne is a speech pathologist with 4+ years' experience across the lifespan, from little ones in early intervention through to adults living with neurological communication challenges. She has a particular passion for Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) and early intervention.

What she loves most about the job is watching someone find their voice, whether that's learning a new way to communicate, building confidence, or feeling able to advocate for themselves. She especially enjoys working alongside culturally and linguistically diverse families, and making sure the support feels accessible, comfortable and meaningful for each one.

Outside of work you'll usually find her doing pilates, reading a book or checking out a new café.

Speaks English, Vietnamese and conversational Chinese.
Qualifications
  • Master of Speech Pathology, University of Melbourne, 2022
  • Certified Practising Speech Pathologist (CPSP), Speech Pathology Australia
  • Hanen More Than Words certified

Between us we speak English, Vietnamese and conversational Chinese, and we're not an NDIS-registered provider, which means we work with self-managed and plan-managed NDIS families as well as private clients.
