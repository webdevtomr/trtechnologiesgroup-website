GROVE HEALTHCARE

We're two Perth speech pathologists who kept asking the same question: why does healthcare so often feel transactional? Kith Grove is our answer, built on connection, compassion and collaboration.

A place where relationships come first, care is person-centred, and families and clinicians feel genuinely supported, valued and connected. This is just the beginning.

Our mission

Kith is an old word for the people you know and trust. A grove is a stand of trees that grow stronger together. We're building exactly that: a community where families get answers, kids find their voices, and clinicians feel backed.

Speech pathology today. Workshops, playgroups, mentoring and a family-clinician community platform as we grow.
