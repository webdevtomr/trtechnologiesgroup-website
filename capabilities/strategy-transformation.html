Does transformation always mean new software?+

No. The right intervention may be a process change, clearer responsibilities, a better customer journey or better use of the systems you already have.

Where does an engagement start?+

With a business priority, a service that needs improvement or a change already underway. We agree the question to answer, the people to involve and the decisions the work needs to support.
