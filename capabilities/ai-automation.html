A few useful answers.

Do we need a custom AI model?+

Not necessarily. The first decision is whether the problem needs AI at all. Existing tools, better integration or a simpler process may be the better fit.

Can we start with one process?+

Yes. A bounded pilot can help establish what is useful before a wider commitment. Agree the scope, data access, human review and success criteria first.

Can you work with our existing technology partners?+

Yes. We can help define the opportunity and coordinate delivery and adoption alongside your internal team and external partners.
