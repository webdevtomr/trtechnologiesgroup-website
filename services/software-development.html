Software & product development

Turn a business problem into a useful application, with the workflows, permissions and operational details designed alongside the interface.

Discuss your project

Where this helps.

A spreadsheet or off-the-shelf tool no longer fits. An idea needs to become a product. Or an existing application needs improvement without losing what already works.

The first release should solve a complete, bounded problem. That means choosing a coherent user journey, not assembling a long list of disconnected features.

What the work involves.

Define the product boundary

Agree the users, core workflow and business constraints. Identify which functions belong in the product and which can remain in existing tools.

Prototype the important decisions

Work through key screens, states, roles and exceptions. Validate the hardest assumptions with realistic content before committing to a full build.

Build in usable increments

Develop the application around agreed acceptance criteria. Keep data handling, access, integrations and error recovery visible throughout delivery.

Prepare it to operate

Plan deployment, support ownership, backups, monitoring and handover for the agreed environment. Prioritise further work using feedback from actual use.

What you leave with.

  • Product scope and prioritised release plan
  • Interactive flows and data / access model
  • Working application with agreed checks
  • Deployment and support handover

Deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed at the start. They are working materials for your team, with the level of detail matched to the decisions and implementation ahead.

Starting together.

A product or process owner, representative users and examples of the work the application needs to support. Existing systems are part of the discovery.

Ongoing support, hosting costs and service levels are agreed separately. We agree how the prototype will be evaluated and what is needed before live use.
