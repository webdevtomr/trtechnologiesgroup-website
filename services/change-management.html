Change management & adoption

Prepare the people affected by change, build the support they need and understand whether the new way of working is taking hold.

Discuss your project

Where this helps.

A system or process is changing, but communication starts late and training is treated as the whole change plan. Teams know something is coming without understanding what it means for their own work.

Assess adoption through changes in everyday work. The plan needs to connect the business outcome with the people, tasks and habits affected.

What the work involves.

Understand the impact

Identify affected groups and what will change in their tasks, decisions and responsibilities. Include the people who support the new process as well as those who use it.

Build the engagement plan

Agree how leaders and managers will explain the change, listen to concerns and make decisions. Tailor communication to the work and needs of each group.

Prepare people to use it

Design practical learning and support around real tasks. Check readiness across process, systems, access, capacity and ownership before rollout.

Follow adoption after launch

Review feedback, support themes and evidence of use. Agree who acts on problems and how the new way of working becomes part of normal operations.

What you leave with.

  • Stakeholder map and change impact assessment
  • Communication and engagement plan
  • Learning, support and readiness activities
  • Adoption measures and post-launch review points

Deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed at the start. They are working materials for your team, with the level of detail matched to the decisions and implementation ahead.

Starting together.

The intended business outcome, affected team representatives and any existing delivery or communication plans.

We agree the approach, activities and deliverables around the people affected and the change ahead.
